Common Questions

A full detail typically takes 3–5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. Trucks and SUVs take longer. We never rush — the job is done when it's done right.
Both options are available. We offer mobile detailing across Montréal and surrounding areas — we come to your home or workplace. Drop-off is also available if you prefer.
Luxury and exotic vehicles require extra care, additional time, and specific pH-neutral products to protect sensitive paint, sensors, and trim. The premium pricing reflects the extra attention your vehicle deserves.
