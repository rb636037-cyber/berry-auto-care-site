Who We Are

Berry Auto Care (legally registered as Soins Automobiles Berry, NEQ 2282286048) is a mobile auto detailing business serving the greater Montreal area. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. Questions? Reach us at berryautocareco@gmail.com or 438-505-7373.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Vehicle details (make, model, year, size)
  • The service you're interested in and any notes you provide

We only collect information you voluntarily give us through our quote form, by text, by phone, or by email.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your quote request and answer questions
  • Schedule and provide detailing services
  • Send appointment reminders and service-related updates
  • Send SMS messages you've opted in to receive (see below)

SMS / Text Messaging

By submitting our quote form and checking the consent box, you agree to receive text messages from Berry Auto Care related to your inquiry, appointments, and offers. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for assistance.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or contact information with any third parties for their marketing purposes. Your consent to receive text messages is never shared with third parties or affiliates. Mobile opt-in data is kept private and used only to communicate with you directly about Berry Auto Care services.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, to understand how visitors use our site, measure the performance of our advertising, and show relevant ads. These technologies may collect information such as your device type, browser, and pages visited. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to request a quote or contact us.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only with service providers that help us operate our business (for example, our messaging and scheduling software), and only to the extent needed to provide our services to you. These providers are bound to keep your information confidential. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal or accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing berryautocareco@gmail.com. You can opt out of text messages by replying STOP, and out of emails using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure, but we work to safeguard the data you share with us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with an updated date.
